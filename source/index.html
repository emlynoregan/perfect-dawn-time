01 / AN IDEA

Time, with a point of view.

We already make clocks agree with geography, but badly: the world is partitioned into irregular political time zones. Perfect Dawn Time turns that convention inside out. Each coordinate has its own clock; its origin is the next actual, calculated sunrise.

Keep ordinary seconds. At each dawn, make one small adjustment to put the hands back at 06:00:00. In most of the inhabited world, the jump is measured in seconds or a minute or two.

A SMALL HISTORY OF THE IDEA

From Continuous Local Time
to Perfect Dawn Time.

My earlier proposal, Continuous Local Time, dispensed with time zones: longitude alone set the clock, continuously, at roughly four minutes per degree. It was local mean solar time without the political boundaries.

But it still privileged noon. Perfect Dawn Time adds latitude, season and the equation of time. Rather than keeping noon near twelve, it keeps dawn at six. Winter and summer afternoons can be very different lengths; the beginning of the day is constant.
